WiFi Tether & Band Switcher

Seamless USB tethering and Wi-Fi band control for jailbroken iPhones and iPads. Share your device's Wi-Fi or VPN with a computer over USB, and keep Wi-Fi on 5 GHz or 2.4 GHz even on routers that won't allow band splitting. The Wi-Fi icon shows which band you're on. USB tethering needs the Share tweak (the app adds its repo); band control works on its own. Rootless, iOS 15+.

The band you're on shows on the Wi-Fi icon at the top (circled), in every app.

Add the repo to Sileo

Or add this source in Sileo by hand: https://scog3.github.io/repo/

Once it's installed, it's called Tether & Band on the home screen.

Wi-Fi band switcher

Open Tether & Band and turn on Force 5 GHz Wi-Fi or Force 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. It works on its own: no other tweaks needed. The first switch after a while can take a few seconds; after that it's near-instant.

USB tethering

  1. Connect the iPhone or iPad to Wi-Fi.
  2. Open Tether & Band and turn on Share Wi-Fi over USB.
  3. Plug it into the computer with a USB cable.

On a Mac that's also on Wi-Fi: System Settings → Network → ⋯ → Set Service Order, and drag iPhone USB above Wi-Fi (or turn the Mac's Wi-Fi off).

Only USB tethering needs the Share tweak: in Tether & Band, tap Add Share's Repo in Sileo, then Add Dist Repo and Add, and install Share. (Or add https://aminerostane.com/repo in Sileo yourself; if Add Source doesn't add it, use Add Dist Repo.)
