WiFi Tether & Band Switcher

Switches that keep a jailbroken iPhone or iPad's Wi-Fi on 5 GHz or 2.4 GHz, even with routers that won't let you split the bands, like BT's Smart Hub 2, Sky's Max Hub, Google Nest Wifi and eero, plus plug-and-play USB tethering of its Wi-Fi or VPN. No SIM or data plan needed, so Wi-Fi-only iPads work too, and nothing to set up on the computer. iPhone and iPad, rootless jailbreaks, iOS 15 and later.

Add the repo to Sileo

Or add this source in Sileo by hand: https://scog3.github.io/repo/

Once it's installed, it's called Tether & Band on the home screen.

Wi-Fi band switcher

Open Tether & Band and turn on Force 5 GHz Wi-Fi or Force 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Only one can be on. It works on its own: no other tweaks needed. The first switch after a while can take a few seconds; after that it's near-instant.

USB tethering

  1. Connect the iPhone or iPad to Wi-Fi.
  2. Open Tether & Band and turn on Share Wi-Fi over USB.
  3. Plug it into the computer with a USB cable.

On a Mac that's also on Wi-Fi: System Settings → Network → ⋯ → Set Service Order, and drag iPhone USB above Wi-Fi (or turn the Mac's Wi-Fi off).

Only USB tethering needs the Share tweak: in Tether & Band, tap Add Share's Repo in Sileo, then Add Dist Repo and Add, and install Share. (Or add https://aminerostane.com/repo in Sileo yourself; if Add Source doesn't add it, use Add Dist Repo.)
